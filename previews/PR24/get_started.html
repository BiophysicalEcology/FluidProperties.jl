Get started ​

FluidProperties.jl calculates physical properties of air, water vapour and liquid water. Inputs and outputs are Unitful.jl quantities.

julia
using Pkg
Pkg.add("FluidProperties")

Dry air ​

dry_air_properties takes the air temperature and pressure:

julia
using FluidProperties, Unitful

air = dry_air_properties(20.0u"°C", 101325.0u"Pa")
DryAirProperties
  density              = 1.19942 kg m^-3
  molar_mass           = 0.0288522 kg mol^-1
  dynamic_viscosity    = 1.81778e-5 kg m^-1 s^-1
  kinematic_viscosity  = 1.51555e-5 m^2 s^-1
  thermal_conductivity = 0.0256576 W K^-1 m^-1
  vapour_diffusivity   = 2.53477e-5 m^2 s^-1
  grashof_coefficient  = 1.45644e8 K^-1 m^-3
  blackbody_emission   = 3.76368e19 J s^-3 c^-2
  peak_wavelength      = 9.88231e-6 m

The fields of the result are the properties:

julia
air.density
1.1994223416150072 kg m^-3
julia
uconvert(u"μPa*s", air.dynamic_viscosity)
18.17782033874917 μPa s

Humid air ​

wet_air_properties also takes the relative humidity, as a fraction:

julia
wet_air_properties(20.0u"°C", 0.5, 101325.0u"Pa")
WetAirProperties
  density                = 1.19539 kg m^-3
  specific_heat          = 1011.09 J kg^-1 K^-1
  vapour_pressure        = 1168.65 Pa
  vapour_density         = 0.00865492 kg m^-3
  mixing_ratio           = 0.00732457
  relative_humidity      = 0.5
  water_potential        = -9.3775e7 Pa
  virtual_temp_increment = 1.28229 K

Atmospheric pressure ​

The pressure at a given elevation can be calculated with atmospheric_pressure:

julia
atmospheric_pressure(1000.0u"m")
89869.07774628018 Pa

Vapour pressure ​

vapour_pressure uses the Goff-Gratch equation unless another equation is given:

julia
vapour_pressure(20.0u"°C")
2337.2941453352582 Pa
julia
vapour_pressure(Huang(), 20.0u"°C")
2339.3207496582745 Pa

Wet bulb temperature ​

wet_bulb_temperature takes the air temperature, relative humidity and pressure:

julia
uconvert(u"°C", wet_bulb_temperature(30.0u"°C", 0.5, 101325.0u"Pa"))
22.16849361243061 °C

Broadcasting ​

All the functions can be broadcast over arrays, including rasters, see Application to rasters:

julia
temperatures = collect(0:10:40) .* u"°C"
uconvert.(u"°C", wet_bulb_temperature.(temperatures, 0.5, 101325.0u"Pa"))
5-element Vector{Quantity{Float64, 𝚯, Unitful.FreeUnits{(K,), 𝚯, Unitful.Affine{-5463//20}}}}:
 -2.928708568136358 °C
  5.514730348503633 °C
 13.815005217967496 °C
 22.16849361243061 °C
 30.740984317658388 °C

Missing values ​

Missing values are propagated:

julia
vapour_pressure(missing)
missing

Related packages ​

Thermodynamics.jl from the Climate Modeling Alliance covers the moist thermodynamics of climate and weather models: energies, entropies, potential temperatures and phase equilibrium, in plain floats with GPU and automatic differentiation support. FluidProperties.jl instead covers the properties needed in biophysical ecology, such as viscosity, conductivity, diffusivity and wet bulb temperatures, with units. The ClausiusClapeyron vapour pressure equation matches Thermodynamics.jl, see Clausius-Clapeyron and Thermodynamics.jl.
